Our Mission

Enriching our community and improving quality of life.

Gaining Our Dignity, Inc. is a charitable nonprofit dedicated to community enrichment. Our purpose is to establish a community center that serves as a resource for people of every age.

Through recreation, education, cultural programming, counseling, and referrals, we aim to strengthen youth, support families, and honor older adults — creating a place where every person is treated with respect and given the opportunity to thrive.

A strong community starts with a place where everyone belongs.

Planned Programs & Services

What the center will offer

Comprehensive services designed to meet the needs of our community.

Recreation

Educational Services

Cultural Programming

Programs that celebrate heritage, the arts, and the diverse traditions of our community.

Counseling

About the Organization

Frequently asked questions

What type of organization is Gaining Our Dignity, Inc.?

Gaining Our Dignity, Inc. is a New York Type B not-for-profit corporation, organized for charitable purposes consistent with Section 501(c)(3) of the Internal Revenue Code.

Is this a religious organization?

No. Gaining Our Dignity, Inc. is not a religious organization.

Will the center operate as a school?

No. The center will provide educational services such as homework supervision, lectures, and seminars, but it will not operate as a school or degree-granting institution.

Who will the center serve?

Our programs are designed for youth, families, and older adults throughout the community.
